# customer-Behavior---retail-sales-analysis-
his project analyzes 99,457 retail transactions worth $251.5M to answer four business questions: where the revenue comes from, who is spending it, how customers pay, and which customers are worth targeting.

Using SQL and DuckDB, I joined sales and customer data and applied aggregations, JOINs, CTEs, CASE WHEN logic, subqueries, and date functions to analyze revenue by category and shopping mall, customer demographics, payment methods, and value-based segmentation.

Key findings:

Clothing generates 45% of total revenue, and Clothing plus Shoes make up about 72%.
Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon together account for about 40% of revenue.
Female customers contribute about 60% of revenue.
Cash accounts for 45% of revenue.
56% of customers fall in the Low Value segment.
